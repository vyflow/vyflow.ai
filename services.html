Website & tracking

The foundation. A site that captures every inquiry with its source.

  • Website redesign on a custom theme: per-clinic pages, condition and service pages, conversion-first forms, fast on phones
  • Server-side lead ledger: every submission recorded with its click ID and posted to the front desk, untouched by ad blockers
  • Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics set up from scratch with the key events that matter: appointment requests, calls, contacts
  • Tracking retrofit for practices that want to keep their current site
  • Website care: hosting, updates, edits, and uptime and form monitoring

Google Ads management

Search campaigns that learn from booked patients, not form fills.

  • Campaigns built and managed for each clinic under the vyflow manager account, with keyword, ad copy, negative-keyword, and budget decisions made by an accountable person
  • Landing pages per condition and service line
  • Offline conversion loop: booked appointments uploaded back to Google Ads by click ID only, so bidding optimizes toward real patients
  • Weekly performance report in plain language, with cost per booked appointment as the headline number
  • Ad spend billed to your own card, never marked up. How our Google Ads API tool works

Local presence

Google Business Profiles for every location, maintained and defended.

  • Audit and cleanup of every listing: categories, hours, services, descriptions, photos, tracked links
  • Agent-maintained listings: posts, holiday hours, and checks for silent changes across all locations
  • Review responses answered daily in the practice's voice, flagged to a human when they should be
  • Location performance folded into the same weekly report as ads and the site

AI operations

Agents that do the recurring upkeep, inside the compliance boundary.

  • Lead follow-up and status agent: front-desk activity flows back into the ledger, so the ads and the report reflect what actually happened
  • Voice agent for after-hours calls, missed calls, and appointment requests
  • Review responder and Business Profile agent across every location
  • Weekly and monthly reports that write themselves
  • Quarterly AI roadmap review: what to automate next, and what not to
