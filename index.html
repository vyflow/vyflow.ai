Client work

One practice, ten clinics, one system.

Sports & Ortho Physical Therapy

Ten-clinic physical therapy group, Chicagoland · Website, tracking, Google Ads, Business Profiles, AI operations

A family-owned practice with ten locations was running a template website, an analytics setup that double-counted, and ten Google Business Profiles that drifted whenever nobody was looking. vyflow rebuilt the site on a custom theme, moved every inquiry to a server-side ledger that ad blockers cannot touch, wired Google Analytics and Google Ads to booked-appointment truth, launched search campaigns, and put agents on the daily upkeep of all ten listings and every review.

  • Custom website launched with per-clinic pages, condition pages, and conversion-first forms
  • Every form submission captured server-side with its click ID, posted straight to the front desk
  • Google Ads live with booked-appointment conversions, managed under the vyflow manager account
  • All ten Business Profiles audited, cleaned, tagged, and now maintained by an agent, with reviews answered daily

Cost per booked evaluation is being measured now that campaigns are live. Numbers will be published here when there are ninety days of them.
