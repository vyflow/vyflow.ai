How it works

Three rules make it trustworthy.

The ledger is truth

Lead counts come from a server-side record that ad blockers cannot touch. Analytics is the map, never the scoreboard, so decisions rest on numbers that are actually complete.

Compliance is structural

For healthcare clients, patient information stays inside BAA-covered systems. Ad platforms receive click IDs and timestamps, nothing that identifies a person. Built in from day one, not patched in later.

Owned, not rented

Clients own their accounts, their data, and their history. vyflow operates the system and hands over clean keys if we ever part ways. No hostage infrastructure.
